Music

Openly licensed music and sound effects from Openverse, each with its licence spelled out; import a track and FableCut keeps its credit for the video's description.

The bin's Music tab and fablecut_music find music and sound effects that may be used in a video: openly licensed tracks from Openverse (openverse.org), which indexes Jamendo, Freesound, ccMixter, Wikimedia Commons and more. Results show every licence, not only the free ones, and each result says what its licence allows. FableCut is made with Openverse but isn't endorsed or certified by it. The licence is the artist's, and Openverse doesn't check it, so the person publishing the video is the one who must respect it.

licencelabelmay it go in a video?
cc0 · pdmfree to useyes, any use; no credit needed
bycredityes, any use; credit the artist
by-sacredit · share alikeyes; credit, and the video is shared under CC BY-SA too
by-nc · by-nc-sacredit · non-commercialnot in ads, sponsored or monetized videos; credit (NC-SA: share alike too)
by-nd · by-nc-ndno editsno: Creative Commons counts music set to video as a derivative
sampling+ · nc-sampling+credit · non-commercialparts may be sampled and remixed; the whole track only non-commercially

Search with words about the music (mood, genre, instrument: "chill lo-fi piano"); leave the words out to see the newest tracks. Music comes from the music category, sound effects from Freesound. Lengths are Openverse's: under 30 s, 30 s – 2 min, 2 – 10 min, over 10 min. Without an account Openverse answers 20 results a page, 240 per search and about 20 searches a minute and 200 a day per address. The search runs from the editor tab (or the MCP server) straight to Openverse, and the file goes through the editor server's URL import into media/.

An imported track keeps its licence on its media entry, so the credits can be listed later:

{ "id": "m_x", "name": "Cinematic — Jahzzar", "kind": "audio", "src": "/media/Cinematic%20-%20Jahzzar.mp3",
  "license": { "code": "by-sa", "label": "CC BY-SA 3.0", "url": "https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/",
    "title": "Cinematic", "creator": "Jahzzar", "page": "https://www.jamendo.com/track/918921",
    "source": "jamendo", "openverse": "c14c7353-…", "attribution": "\"Cinematic\" by Jahzzar is licensed under …" } }

The credits are every licensed file the edit uses (main timeline, comps and other sequences; a denoised copy counts as its original), one line each: "Cinematic" by Jahzzar (jamendo) — CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://…) — https://www.jamendo.com/track/918921, with a warning for a licence that limits the video (non-commercial, share alike, no derivatives). They go in the video's description.

In the editor: the bin's Music tab has the search box, a length, OK to monetize (only licences that allow commercial use and edits), and mood chips to start from. The SFX tab has the same search for sound effects (with chips such as whoosh, impact, riser) above the files in library/sfx/. Each result shows its artist, length, genres, source, a licence badge (green: free to use, blue: credit, amber: non-commercial, red: no edits; hover for what it allows, click for the licence) and a link to its page. ▶ previews it, ⬇ imports it into the Project bin, ＋ (or a double-click) imports it and adds it at the playhead. More results loads the next page. A file's Media info shows its licence and credit with Copy credit, and the Export dialog lists the edit's music credits with Copy. Importing needs the editor server (or Studio).

Agents use fablecut_music:

  • {query, kind?:"music"|"sfx", length?:"shortest"|"short"|"medium"|"long", commercial?, edits?, licenses?:[…], page?} searches: 20 tracks a page, each id "title" — artist · m:ss · licence [flags] · genres · source. commercial:true keeps licences that allow commercial use, edits:true leaves out ND; pass both when the video will be monetized.
  • {import:"<track id>"} downloads it into media/ (named "Title - Artist") and registers it with its license; the reply gives the licence, the credit and an addClip to place it. Then fablecut_beats {clipId} finds its tempo, retime cuts to it, and fablecut_auto_duck dips it under speech.
  • {credits:true} lists the edit's credits and licence warnings. fablecut_export adds them to its reply when the edit uses licensed music.
  • Say which licence a track has before the user publishes, and search with commercial:true, edits:true when they plan to monetize. Import what the user picked, or the best fit when they asked you to choose. The compact view shows an imported file's licence:"CC BY 3.0".

Recipes: music under a reel - search the mood with length:"short", import one, place it on A1, fablecut_beats {clipId, retime:{every:2}}; a whoosh on every cut - kind:"sfx", query:"whoosh", import a CC0 one, place it just before each cut; music for a sponsored video - commercial:true, edits:true, and give the user the credits from the reply.
